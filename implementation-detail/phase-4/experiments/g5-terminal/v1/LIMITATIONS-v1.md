# G5 terminal limitations

G5 is terminal PASS for the narrowed benchmark-mechanism contract. It is not a
production, SDK, VFS, portability, or hostile-filesystem qualification.

- G5-1 decision latencies are `CacheWarmPreconditionedNotColdReopen`.
  Preconditioning is charged in complete campaign wall but is outside the
  product timer to match the retained G4 custody read.
- Controlled-cold OS/device residency and physical I/O remain Unavailable.
- `Verified` remains the default. `TrustedLocalDev` is Store-lifetime and does
  not provide rollback freshness; rollback freshness remains `NotProtected`.
- G5-1 RSS is per product child. Aggregate multi-child RSS is not claimed.
- Fixed-radix count-changing edits remain suffix-linear. File-size-insensitive
  count change under a new canonical representation is deferred.
- G5-2 performance is an exact 250,000-byte warm mechanism claim. Different
  length/position-changing edits are correct `FullFallback`; they are not
  presented as a fast route or a large-file claim.
- G5-2 is benchmark-private, one worker, one in-flight request, one bounded
  replaceable pending Latest request, and a process-lifetime native seed.
- The projection cleanup pathname race is accepted only for the declared
  private, nonadversarial environment. There is no malicious same-UID claim.
- G5-3 is one 1 MiB, 1,000-revision same-size history in one child and one
  10 MiB two-reader/one-writer sentinel. It is not a random-history,
  multi-file, multi-worker, multi-process scaling, backup, or restore claim.
- Reachability is read-only observation. Append-only storage remains and no
  destructive GC or deletion is implemented.
- G5-3 reuses G5-2 exact/latest, fault, shutdown, and restart authority rather
  than rerunning those populations at every history revision.
- No 500 MiB campaign, WAL, retry loop, worker pool, persistent queue,
  production extraction, VFS/SDK integration, G6 implementation, or WP5 work
  is included.
- Forty-nine `__pycache__` directories remain inside preserved historical
  attempt trees. They are non-authoritative generated residue, excluded from
  every controlling manifest, and are neither method nor evidence authority;
  they were preserved rather than deleting historical trees casually.

Frozen temporal text that predates screen/gate is preserved as history. Later
terminal artifacts supersede it without rewriting the frozen method. The G5-0
historical method manifest names two shared source paths that later evolved;
its immutable result package rehashes exactly, and G5-1/2/3 freeze the later
source bytes explicitly.
