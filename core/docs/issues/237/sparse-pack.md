## Mechanism and custody

`core/crates/layerfs-storage/src/policy.rs:110` fixes `PACK_LIMIT` at 262,144 B. `core/crates/layerfs-storage/src/pack/layout.rs:43-51` puts a little-endian assembled length at byte offset 16 of a v9+ pack header; `:261-275` reserves the whole pack limit for every nonsingleton lane. `core/crates/layerfs-storage/src/pack/placement.rs:226-265` carries both `used` and `capacity` into `SelectedWrite`; `core/crates/layerfs-storage/src/sqlite/write.rs:81-101` creates `zeroblob(capacity)` and `:130-167` writes the body, directory, and control area through incremental BLOB I/O. Thus `sum(length(data))` counts reservation, while `sum(header.used)` counts assembled bytes, **including** the reserved directory region. Neither is pure payload bytes.

## Minimum prospective control/treatment check for an Init change

1. Freeze and record one source/corpus manifest, route, seed, environment, cache state, timer, product/harness/binary seals, and numeric space limit before runs. For public native Init, use the same real API and fixture in each arm; keep the 10k and 100k results separate. Init is a fresh-output case. Do one performance sample per arm/case in distinct output directories, retain failures, and do not repeat an arm to choose a time. Follow `core/benchmark/fs-bench-pro/AGENTS.md:16-22,69-101` and root `AGENTS.md` §3.
2. In a separate child, **reopen each Store** and compare the full expected manifest against actual path presence, kind, metadata, size, and SHA-256 of every file; confirm the C5 root and cleanup. `core/benchmark/fs-bench-pro/AGENTS.md:30-43,60-65` requires an explicit full verifier for #231. The legacy history verifier in `core/benchmark/fs-bench-pro-storage-content/src/ops/history.rs:1370-1375,1433-1450,1562-1593` samples paths and marks such a result `INCOMPLETE`; its sampled result cannot substitute for the requested full sparse-history readback.
3. From each **closed** Store record `st_size`, `st_blocks*512`, `sum(length(data))`, pack count, per-version/lane count, `sum(header.used)`, capacity minus used, and utilization distribution. Pin `FULL`, `PREFIX`, trial, inserted/reused, and dedup counters, plus all canonical roots. Require treatment apparent and allocated Store bytes, pack capacity, and spare bytes not to exceed its same-policy control's prospectively frozen limits; any changed representation choice needs explicit explanation and full readback. A root-only match cannot waive a space regression.
4. Run a separate sparse-history guardrail with the **same** `history-stride1` corpus and exactly the same advisory/depth switches, product policy, and harness in each arm. Verify all 157 roots **and all paths** after reopen, and compare the same physical metrics. The retained stride1 operations already exceed the repository's 15/25 s complete-command budget (`stage-6-history-209-merged-20260920T225623Z/README.md:14-28`; `/tmp/hs1-fixed/timing.json`), so label any such exploration as a diagnostic and report the performance/admission row `NOT_RUN` unless a qualifying identity-matched receipt can be reused. Do not shrink the history or raise its timeout to manufacture a gate.

The archived Store is available for structural diagnosis, but its depth and representation differ. A true #229 matched-policy baseline, and full reopened readback receipts for these two historical Stores, remain unavailable in the named artifacts. Dense Init Store compactness cannot stand in for this sparse-history guardrail.
