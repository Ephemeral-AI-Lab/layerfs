## 1. How the baseline is selected

A baseline is a prospectively declared source and method, not the fastest run.
This inventory records the complete retained Workspace cohort immediately
available before the proposed integration/reorganization: all nine cells at
one product, workload and harness identity. No row is selected from a different
iteration to improve a number. The independent #248 row keeps its own fixture.

For the historical before-#273 algorithm comparison, the frozen control is
`48b51e874a41b3e1e6c6661e145316df8b408f07`, tree
`64d06dabba1a6d123d1002487c189809ded05cc0`. Its matched measurements are
**NOT_RUN**. There is therefore no qualified speed baseline or speedup ratio.
The current candidate cohort below is a **pre-integration engineering reference**,
with every numeric row still **INELIGIBLE**. It is not renamed as the frozen
control or relabeled performance PASS.

Init and history use their own retained cohorts and surfaces. Release/debug,
full/lite verification, SDK/FUSE/C1-C2, selected-history policies and dates
are not pooled. A future matched comparison must freeze its control, candidate
and common method before either arm runs. One sample per selected case/arm;
no fastest-run selection, best-of, warm replacement or resampling of an unchanged
arm. The later harness layout needs a new seal without changing old receipts.
