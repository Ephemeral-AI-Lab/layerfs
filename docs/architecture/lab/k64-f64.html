K64/F64: compact reads, suffix-sensitive edits

Every leaf holds at most 64 payload occurrences. Every branch holds at most 64 children. Parent-relative cumulative ends route reads efficiently, while fixed ordinal partitioning makes count-changing edits suffix-sensitive.
