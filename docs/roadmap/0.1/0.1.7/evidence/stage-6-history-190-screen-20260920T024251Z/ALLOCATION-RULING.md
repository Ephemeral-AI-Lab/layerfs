# Owner allocation tradeoff ruling

> Status: Owner-directed disposition for this investigation; historical measurements stay unchanged.

The owner stated: “small miss of storage allocation is accepted if it bring good time reduction.” Apply this as a conditional acceptance of small allocation overages when accompanied by a worthwhile measured time reduction. The owner's preceding instruction establishes that a one-second saving is worthwhile. This is not an unlimited storage waiver and does not change raw byte counts, old receipts, cold-cache eligibility or missing history counter pins.

This candidate remains rejected because its operation difference is only 156,679,583 ns and its targeted phase difference is 53,181,962 ns. Its storage overages, 24,576 and 303,104 bytes, are not an independent reason to reject it. They stay reported as numeric target misses, with this owner disposition beside them. If a future candidate produces a worthwhile measured speedup and a small allocation overage, the overage alone is not a blocker under this ruling.
